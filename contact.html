Before You Reach Out

Common questions

Q

What happens in an initial consultation?

An initial consultation is a substantive working session — typically 60–90 minutes — where we review your current situation, understand your objectives, and assess how our practice areas apply to your needs. We will ask direct questions and give you direct answers. No pitch deck. No obligation at the end.

Q

Do you work with clients outside the United States?

Yes. A significant portion of our client base has cross-border wealth, multi-jurisdictional tax obligations, or business interests across multiple countries. We are experienced in cross-border planning and work with local counsel in relevant jurisdictions where needed.

Q

How does Fin Astra charge for its services?

Our fee structure depends on the nature and scope of the engagement. We offer retainer-based advisory relationships for ongoing clients, fixed-fee engagements for defined projects (e.g. trust drafting, compliance reviews), and hourly consultations for discrete advice. All fees are disclosed in writing at engagement. We do not accept commissions or referral fees from third-party product providers.

Q

Is our communication protected by attorney-client privilege?

Upon formal engagement, communications with Fin Astra's licensed attorneys in the course of providing legal advisory services may be protected by attorney-client privilege under applicable law. We will clarify the scope of privilege as it applies to your engagement at the outset. This website communication does not establish an attorney-client relationship.
