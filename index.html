How We Work With You

Client confidentiality is not a feature — it is the foundation

Every engagement at Fin Astra is governed by attorney-client privilege standards. Your financial affairs, disclosed in the context of our advisory relationship, receive the same level of protection as confidential legal communications.

We do not share, sell, or reference client information without explicit consent. Our professionals are bound by bar ethics rules — not merely contractual non-disclosures — making the standard of confidentiality here materially higher than a standard financial advisory relationship.

What our clients can expect

  • → Attorney-client privilege applied to all advisory communications
  • → Written engagement letters defining scope and protections
  • → Dedicated primary contact — no rotating associates
  • → No referral fees or third-party product commissions
  • → Transparent fee structure disclosed at engagement
