Practice Areas

Our Practice Areas

01

Wealth & Estate Structuring

Wealth preservation is not simply an investment question — it is a legal one. At Fin Astra, wealth and estate structures are drafted by attorneys who understand both the financial objective and the enforceability of every provision.

We work with high-net-worth individuals, family offices, and multi-generational wealth holders to design, implement, and maintain structures that protect assets against liability, tax inefficiency, and succession uncertainty.

Discuss Your Situation →

What's Included

  • Revocable and irrevocable trust drafting and administration
  • Asset protection structuring against litigation and creditors
  • Multi-jurisdictional estate coordination
  • Family office governance frameworks
  • Charitable giving structures (donor-advised funds, CRTs)
  • Business interest transfer and valuation planning
  • Life insurance policy analysis within estate context
02

Regulatory & Compliance Advisory

Regulatory non-compliance is not a technical failure — it is a legal exposure. Our compliance advisory is grounded in a thorough understanding of applicable law, not just industry best practice.

We advise individuals, financial services firms, investment advisers, and registered entities on their regulatory obligations — and design systems to meet them, ahead of enforcement.

Discuss Your Situation →

What's Included

  • Investment adviser registration with SEBON and Nepal Rastra Bank
  • SEBON and Nepal Rastra Bank compliance programme design
  • AML/KYC framework implementation
  • Annual compliance programme review and testing
  • Regulatory change monitoring and impact assessment
  • Enforcement response and remediation planning
  • Cross-border reporting obligations (FBAR, FATCA, CRS)
03

Tax Strategy & Planning

Tax planning is most effective when it is proactive, structurally sound, and legally defensible. Our tax strategy practice is led by attorneys with advanced tax qualifications who design plans that work in the real regulatory environment.

We do not recommend aggressive positions. We identify every legitimate efficiency available within the law and structure your affairs to take advantage of them — documented to withstand scrutiny.

Discuss Your Situation →

What's Included

  • Individual and family income tax planning
  • Capital gains management and deferral strategies
  • Qualified opportunity zone and 1031 exchange advisory
  • Business entity selection and restructuring
  • Transfer pricing and international tax planning
  • Estate and gift tax strategy
  • Coordination with external CPAs and tax preparers
04

Investment Structuring & Due Diligence

Investment decisions are made under pressure, with incomplete information, and with significant legal implications that most advisors are not equipped to identify. We bring legal-grade diligence to every opportunity we review on your behalf.

Our approach: document, analyse, structure. We identify the legal, regulatory, and contractual risks in any proposed investment before a commitment is made — and structure your participation to limit exposure.

Discuss Your Situation →

What's Included

  • Legal and financial due diligence on private investments
  • Review of subscription agreements and offering documents
  • SPV and holding structure design
  • Portfolio governance and investor rights analysis
  • Real estate investment structuring and review
  • Alternative asset class advisory (private equity, debt)
  • Exit structuring and secondary transaction review
05

Corporate & Business Financial Advisory

For business owners and executives, the separation between company finances and personal wealth is often more permeable than it should be. Our corporate advisory practice addresses both — with the legal rigour that governance demands.

We serve founder-led businesses, private companies, and family enterprises navigating capital events, restructurings, and M&A — advising on the financial and legal dimensions simultaneously.

Discuss Your Situation →

What's Included

  • Capital structure analysis and optimisation
  • M&A financial advisory and transaction support
  • Business valuation for planning and transaction purposes
  • Equity compensation plan design and review
  • Financial risk governance frameworks
  • Debt structuring and lender negotiation support
  • Board-level financial reporting and governance
06

Trust & Succession Planning

Succession planning fails most frequently not because of financial miscalculation — but because the legal instruments are inadequate, ambiguous, or untested against the actual complexity of the family or business situation.

Our attorney-advisors design succession structures that are legally robust, tax-aware, and practically workable — and we revisit them as circumstances change, because succession is not a one-time document.

Discuss Your Situation →

What's Included

  • Comprehensive succession strategy and timeline
  • Trust drafting and trustee selection advisory
  • Business succession and owner buy-sell agreements
  • Family governance and council frameworks
  • GRAT, IDGT, and advanced transfer planning strategies
  • Beneficiary readiness and financial education programmes
  • Incapacity planning and power of attorney structures
