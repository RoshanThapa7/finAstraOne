Our Story

Why lawyers built a financial advisory firm

Fin Astra was founded in 2008 by a group of attorneys who had spent years watching the same pattern repeat itself: clients received financial advice that was technically competent and legally naive. The advice worked in a spreadsheet. It failed in practice — against tax codes, regulatory requirements, and the fine print of the structures that were supposed to protect people.

The founding partners made a deliberate choice to build something different — not a law firm that dabbled in financial planning, and not a financial planning firm that occasionally consulted attorneys. A single integrated practice, where legal training and financial expertise are held in the same hands, applied simultaneously, for every client and every recommendation.

What began as a boutique practice serving a handful of high-net-worth families has grown into a full-service advisory firm with a client base spanning individuals, family offices, and private businesses across twelve jurisdictions. The founding principle has not changed: your financial strategy should be able to withstand scrutiny — legal, regulatory, and fiduciary — at every level.

What We Stand For

Our values

01

Legal Integrity First

Every recommendation we make is evaluated against applicable law before it is evaluated against financial return. We do not design plans that work only if enforcement stays asleep.

02

Fiduciary Without Exception

We hold ourselves to fiduciary standards for every client, on every matter, without qualification. No client is too small for our best judgment. No fee arrangement changes that standard.

03

Precision in Language

We say exactly what we mean — in our advice, our documents, and our communications. Vague financial guidance is not caution; it is a liability. We prefer clarity, even when the answer is uncomfortable.

04

Long-Term Perspective

We do not optimise for short-term outcomes at the expense of long-term structural soundness. Our recommendations are designed to hold — legally, financially, and relationally — over time.
